About Us!

Congratulations to Dev/Mission for facilitating its 26th Cohort! Within 10 weeks, Cohort #26 has accomplished many tech objectives in IoT, IT, Programming and Critical career skills. There's one thing that this Cohort makes itself stand out from the previous ones. That is that this cohort has been the one with the most women participating in the preapprenticeship!

Every student in this cohort has made a sacrifice to show up four days of out the week to learn the skills needed to break into the tech industry. Every student in this cohort sacrificed their summer time just to be here. Now thats dedication.

For the ones that made it through the graduation, keep the momentum going and may we all enjoy the fruits of our labor.
